# Initial Concept
EventFlow - Modern Event Management System
# Product Definition: EventFlow

## Vision
EventFlow is a comprehensive, modern event management platform designed to provide a seamless and highly interactive experience for event organizers, attendees, corporate planners, community leaders, and venue managers. It leverages real-time features and a polished UI to simplify the complexities of organizing and attending events.

## Target Audience
- **Event Organizers:** From professional planners to community group leaders.
- **Attendees:** Individuals looking for seamless discovery and booking.
- **Corporate Event Planners:** Businesses managing large-scale internal or external events.
- **Venue Managers:** Facilities looking to list spaces and manage event logistics.

## Core Features & Management Tools
- **Advanced Management Suite:**
    - **Analytics Dashboard:** Real-time tracking of sales, attendee demographics, and engagement metrics.
    - **Attendee Communication:** Automated email marketing and integrated communication tools.
    - **Resource Management:** Tools for managing event resources and inventory.
- **Seamless Booking System:** One-click registration and flexible management.
- **Social & Community Building:**
    - **Follow System:** Users can follow their favorite organizers, venues, and recurring events.
    - **Interactive Forums:** Dedicated discussion spaces for each event.
    - **Direct Messaging:** Secure user-to-user communication for networking.

## Revenue Model
- **Transaction-Based:** Commissions or service fees on ticket sales.
- **Promotion & Visibility:** Featured placements for events and advertising opportunities for organizers and venues.

## Tech Stack (Confirmed)
- **Frontend:** Next.js 15 (App Router), React 19, TypeScript.
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, Radix UI, Shadcn/UI.
- **Backend:** Supabase (PostgreSQL, Auth, Storage, Real-time, Edge Functions).
